# Accessibility

Last Updated: {new Date(frontmatter.lastModified).toLocaleString()}

Ensembles, Inc. is committed to making music and art available to everyone in
our community, including people with disabilities. This page describes the
accessibility of our website and our building, and how to request help if you
run into a barrier.

## Website

We aim to make [charlestownensembles.com](https://charlestownensembles.com)
usable with a range of browsers, devices, and assistive technologies. We work
toward Level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
([WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)).

We have not completed a formal third-party audit, so we do not claim full
conformance. We review the site ourselves as we add and update pages.

### What we do

- Provide a responsive layout that works on phones, tablets, and desktops
- Use semantic HTML, page titles, and heading structure
- Label form fields and keep visible focus styles on interactive controls
- Include text alternatives for meaningful images
- Support keyboard navigation for core tasks such as browsing activities,
  registering, and contacting us

### Limitations

Some content is provided by third parties (for example, payment, sign-in, maps,
or social media embeds). We do not control the accessibility of those tools.

If a page, form, or document is difficult to use, please tell us. We will work
with you to provide the information another way when we can.

## Our building

Most classes and events are held at:

**Charlestown Ensembles**
1120 Monroe St.
Charlestown, IN 47111

The front entrance is step-free from the sidewalk. There are no stairs inside
the building, and the restroom is accessible.

If you need an accommodation to take part in a class, performance, or other
event, or if you have questions about parking or seating, please contact us
before you visit. We will do our best to help.

Some activities take place at other locations. When that happens, the activity
page will list the venue. Reach out if you need information about that site.
