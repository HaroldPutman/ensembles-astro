Make art for holiday celebrations from around the world, and take a new project home each week.

Students paint Eric Carle–style menorahs, shape gingerbread ornaments from salt dough, build
paper-plate poinsettias, construct a dragon for Chinese New Year, craft Scandinavian gnomes, and
draw Christmas lights in chalk. Each Tuesday is a new place and a project they can show off.
