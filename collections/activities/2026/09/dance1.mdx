Dance 2 is a class that is going to explore multiple dance techniques, styles of dance and
performance skills. This session will be focused on Lyrical dance style. Lyrical is a soft
expressive dance style conveying emotions of hurt, love, longing, and compassion. Lyrical
uses technical skills of ballet and blends them with the fluid style of Jazz and contemporary
dance. This style of dance is a way of emotional story telling that will tug at the heart strings.
Class will consist of warm-up exercises, technique, learning set choreography and a performance
on the last day of class. 

**Dress code:** 
- Dance shoes: Preferably Jazz shoes (Ballet shoes or character shoes will also work)
NO street shoes (tennis shoes, sandals, flipflops, boots etc…)
- Athletic wear: Jazz pants, leggings, athletic tops, Leotards are optional  

_This class is a safe place for ALL dancers, whether you are new or
experienced. It will be a lighthearted, energetic place to learn, enjoy, and
experience the world of dance with no judgment. ALL are welcome and
encouraged to join the beautiful world of dance!_
